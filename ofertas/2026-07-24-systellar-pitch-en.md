# Systellar — guion de 2 min en inglés (screening call)

Frases cortas, vocabulario B2. Ensayar en voz alta 3-4 veces; no memorizar palabra a palabra, memorizar los 5 bloques.

## 1. Opening (15s)
"Hi, I'm Victor. I'm a Senior AI Engineer based in Barcelona. I work at WeAi, where I build LLM agents and copilots in production for enterprise clients like BBVA, Iberia and Telefónica."

## 2. What I do (40s)
"My day-to-day is very close to your job description. I design multi-agent systems on Claude: RAG over vector stores, tool use, and orchestration between agents. I care a lot about reliability, so I build automated evaluation pipelines — we measure agent quality, we don't assume it. And I own the full path: Python and FastAPI backends, PostgreSQL, React and TypeScript on the frontend, Docker and CI/CD on Azure and GCP."

## 3. Agentic development (30s)
"Agentic development is not a buzzword for me — it's my daily workflow. I use Claude Code every day, I'm certified by Anthropic in MCP — the introduction and the advanced course — and I've built my own MCP tools. I review and own everything the agent produces. I can defend every line, which I believe is exactly what your technical challenge is about."

## 4. Why Systellar (20s)
"I love the thesis: agents transformed software, hardware is next, and the missing piece is structured engineering context. That is an MCP-shaped problem, and it's the kind of hard, real-world engineering I want to own. Also: I'm local — Spanish and Catalan are my native languages — and I have full EU work rights and NATO-state citizenship."

## 5. Honest close (15s)
"To be transparent: I have four intense years shipping production systems, not ten. What I bring is true end-to-end ownership — I've built complete systems alone, from data model to deployment — plus a level of agentic-engineering fluency that is hard to find. I'd rather show you than tell you."

## Respuestas preparadas (probables repreguntas)

**"You have 4 years, we asked for 6-10."**
"That's fair. I'd say judge the ownership, not the years: at 4 years I've already been the technical reference for AI at a consultancy serving BBVA-scale clients, and earlier I built an industrial ERP completely alone. Your own JD says you don't expect an exact checklist match — I think my agentic depth is where I'm ahead of the market."

**"How deep is your TypeScript?"**
"My strongest backend language is Python. I use TypeScript and React on the frontend regularly, and Node on the backend when the project needs it. With agentic tooling I move fast across stacks — and I understand and challenge every line before it ships." *(No exagerar más que esto.)*

**"Are you comfortable with defense work?"**
→ Decide TU respuesta antes de la llamada. Su marco: solo proyectos alineados con sus líneas rojas, intereses de la UE.

**"Salary?"**
"The range you published, 60 to 75, works for me. Around 65 feels right for what I bring, and I'm flexible with the equity mix."

## Tips
- Screening = cómo piensas y cómo suenas. Ritmo tranquilo > acento perfecto.
- Ten a mano 1 ejemplo concreto de agente en producción (arquitectura, evals, fallo real que arreglaste).
- Ellos comparten el historial de conversación con IA en la prueba: menciona que trabajas así a diario — es tu mejor carta.
