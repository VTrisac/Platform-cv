🚀 About Murphy AI

Murphy AI is revolutionizing debt collection through conversational artificial intelligence. We're building an advanced debt recovery platform where AI agents conduct natural, empathetic conversations across multiple channels—with a special focus on voice interactions at scale.

Our AI agents don't just send emails or SMS. They pick up the phone, understand human speech, respond intelligently, and negotiate—all while maintaining the nuance and empathy that complex financial conversations require. We're talking about real-time speech processing, emotional intelligence, and conversational flows that adapt on the fly.

The challenge? Making our agents sound so natural that debtors forget they're talking to AI, while handling the complexity of financial negotiations, regulatory compliance, and emotional conversations—and making the system increasingly more intelligent with every interaction.
